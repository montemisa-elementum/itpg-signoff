1 · The two buckets

Demand Request and Portfolio Item

These two Elementum apps carry the whole process. A request enters as demand; if it needs funding or governance it becomes a portfolio item, and the same identifier follows it to the end.

App

Demand Request

handle DMD · namespace demand-request · wave 1

Every technology ask lands here — business requests, technical debt, and security remediation alike. Its job is to end with a recorded decision, not to become a project.

  1. Capture — one form that changes by demand type; drafts can be saved and resumed.
  2. Validate — submit enforces the mandatory set, so triage does not start on half a request.
  3. Route — the affected product determines the accountable owner automatically.
  4. Screen — check open demand, in-flight items, and the engineering backlog for overlap.
  5. Dispose — route to backlog, accept as investment, merge, defer, reject, or redirect, with a reason.

Most demand ends here, on an existing product team's backlog. That is the intended outcome, not a failure.

App

Portfolio Item

handle PFI · namespace portfolio-item · waves 2–3

The funded bet. One record holds the business case, the decision, the baseline, and delivery health — so nothing is retyped into a second system after approval.

  1. Qualify — structured case: options, cost by year, benefit owner, measurable outcomes.
  2. Score — the published scoring model ranks it against everything else, including mandatory work.
  3. Decide — value and type select the approval authority; conditions are recorded, not remembered.
  4. Baseline — approved funding is locked, which is what makes later variance meaningful.
  5. Execute — cadence, health with mandatory commentary, milestones, and control gates with evidence.
  6. Realize — outcomes stay open after delivery closes until the value is measured.

2 · What hangs off them

Everything else supports one of the two

We think of the remaining apps as “child apps”. That is our shorthand for how they behave, not an Elementum feature — each one is an ordinary app with a relation back to its parent record.

Why they are separate apps

A supporting object gets its own app when it repeats or needs its own owner and dates. A portfolio item has many cost lines, several conditions, and a list of milestones — none of which fit in a single field.

What stays a field instead

The demand disposition is a status plus a reason. Health rating and narrative are fields. Status history is native. The source data inventory listed these as separate tables; most should not become apps.

Optional modules

Capacity, outcomes, dependencies, and vendor engagement are selectable modules rather than core. Each needs its own decision and, in some cases, an integration before it is worth deploying.

6 · Core vs tenant

What is reusable, what is yours

The apps, fields, and rules are the product. Your estate, labels, thresholds, and integrations are configuration records loaded after deployment, so the next organization reuses the same build.

Product core — same for every customer
  • Demand and Portfolio Item lifecycles and field sets
  • A persistent identifier carried across related records
  • Structured business case and mandatory baseline lock
  • Gate, scoring, and authority mechanisms driven by element records
  • Cadence rules, health commentary, exception views
Tenant pack — records, not code
  • Portfolio hierarchy, products, platforms, services
  • Authority labels (for example L0–L3) and value bands
  • Gate criteria and scoring weights per investment type
  • Group membership and who can see financials
  • CloudLinks and table mapping for Jira/ADO, ERP, identity, sourcing

7 · Exclusions

Not built in this specification

Stated plainly so acceptance criteria are not written against something we are not delivering.

Portfolio dependency graph

Delivered as registers, filters, and most-constrained-function views. A drawn network of every dependency is not in scope.

Scenario optimizer

Scenarios are include and exclude with roll-ups. No solver proposes a portfolio, and no model is simulated against the whole book.

BI replacement

List, kanban, and exception views replace the manual pack. Heat maps and executive dashboards stay in the BI tool.

Second master data

No vendor master, employee master, or general ledger. Those stay external table ports.

Spreadsheet round-trip

The business case is structured fields. We do not parse an uploaded workbook as the system of record.

Autonomous decisions

No agent sets a disposition, board outcome, gate pass, or realized value.
