Access

Client membership, product policy

The product defines what each role can do; the client nominates who holds the role and the organizational scope in which it applies.

Role groups

RequestersProduct OwnersPlatform OwnersEngineering LeadsArchitectsSecurity ReviewersFinance PartnersBenefit OwnersInvestment BoardGovernance Admins

Policy intent

  • Requesters see their own demand, not portfolio financials
  • Financials and board papers are restricted by role and organization
  • Vendor commercials are restricted to sourcing and accountable owners
  • Gate evidence and waivers are visible to control reviewers
  • Only governance administrators publish model changes

Agents

Assistive capabilities, after trustworthy records exist

Every application has AI requirements, but agents are not a prerequisite for the deterministic process.

Demand & case

Draft summaries, suggest classification/alignment, present duplicate candidates, and challenge unsupported case assumptions.

Execution & value

Draft status and governance packs, challenge RAG against evidence, chase overdue measurements and draft post-implementation reviews.

Dependencies & vendors

Detect unstated dependencies, warn of emerging risk, summarize vendor performance and identify overlap candidates.

Human decision boundary: agents do not approve investments, pass gates, accept dependencies, validate realized value or record vendor-deliverable acceptance.
