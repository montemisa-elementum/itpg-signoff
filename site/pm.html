Executive brief

The change in one view

This engagement is tenant-0 for a generic Elementum ITPG product. The process is reusable; each organization supplies its own products, approval bands, roles, and integrations.

Today

Fragmented evidence

Requests, business cases, milestones, status, and benefits are split across seven or more tools and manually reconciled.

Target

One governed spine

A request is routed to a backlog or receives a Portfolio ID that connects decision, execution, cost, dependencies, and value.

Principle

Integrate, do not duplicate

Elementum owns the process. Jira/ADO, ERP, directory, cloud billing, and vendor-risk systems remain systems of record.

Important: most demand should go to an existing product backlog. Only work requiring new funding or governance follows the full investment path.

Product boundary

Reusable core vs tenant configuration

The distinction prevents a tenant-specific requirement from distorting the product for every future customer.

Product core
  • Demand and Portfolio Item lifecycles
  • Persistent Portfolio ID
  • Structured case and baseline
  • Config-driven gates, scoring, and DoA
  • Execution cadence and exception views
  • Automations commit; agents suggest
Tenant pack
  • Product hierarchy and named objectives
  • L0–L3 or local authority labels
  • Jira, ADO, ERP, IdP, and TPRM mappings
  • Groups, membership, and access scope
  • Gate criteria and scoring weights
  • Customer-specific UAT and seed records
Not promised in v1: a portfolio-wide dependency graph, AI selecting the portfolio, replacing engineering/vendor/finance systems of record, or custom BI heat maps.
